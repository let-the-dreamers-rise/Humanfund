01The problem already on your desk

You are about to hand out Phase 3 builder grants. How do you allocate them fairly? An opaque committee invites capture and favouritism claims — the exact criticism aimed at every foundation that funds its own ecosystem. The gold-standard answer is a community-weighted quadratic funding round (Gitcoin's mechanism): the crowd decides, and breadth of support beats size of wallet.

There is one catch, and it has blocked quadratic funding for a decade: it is trivially sybil-attackable. Split one budget across many wallets and you multiply your own match. Gitcoin and Human Passport spend millions on sybil defense to patch this, and still leak. You are the one chain where that defense is free and structural — because every account is already a verified unique human.

And the demand is not speculative. Sybil-proof funding is one of the most validated needs in the space: Human Passport has secured $512M+ in capital flow across 120+ projects, Story Protocol used it to protect a $98M airdrop, and Gitcoin has run community funding rounds for years. The appetite is enormous and proven — what has never existed is a chain where the defense is free instead of a millions-of-dollars arms race. That chain is yours, and the first customer is you.

02What HumanFund is

A sybil-proof funding and allocation platform for InterLink: quadratic funding rounds and fair distribution where one human is one vote, enforced by the chain, not approximated by a scoring heuristic.

  • Sybil-proof by construction. The attack that breaks Gitcoin cannot run here — one human cannot become a hundred contributors.
  • Transparent and on-chain. Every contribution and every match is public and verifiable — the opposite of a closed committee spreadsheet.
  • Reusable by the whole ecosystem. Once it runs your grants, any project on the chain can run a sybil-proof round, airdrop, or community vote on the same rails.
  • Reputation that compounds. Because a human can't start over, honest participation builds an inescapable, portable reputation — the second product on the same primitive.

04Why it's yours to fund, not to build in-house

A foundation that both funds its ecosystem and runs the allocation invites exactly the capture claim you want to avoid. An independent, transparent, sybil-proof allocator is more credible precisely because it isn't you — the same reason Gitcoin sits outside the chains it funds. And it depends on a personhood read you control: you keep the leverage, you fund a neutral rail you could not credibly operate yourself, and it becomes public infrastructure every project on the chain reuses. World ID cannot enter this — it lives on other chains and forbids the persistent identity this needs.

07Sustainability — not grant-dependent

The grant funds the first round; the chain's own funding activity sustains the platform. Three revenue lines:

  • Run-a-round-as-a-service — a small platform fee on matching pools that third-party projects and DAOs run through HumanFund.
  • Sybil-proof distribution — fair airdrops and allocations for launches that want guaranteed one-human-one-claim.
  • Reputation API — the compounding contribution reputation, consumed by lenders, DAOs, and other rounds on the chain.

If funding stopped after M4, HumanFund keeps running on the rounds the ecosystem puts through it.
